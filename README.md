# Atliq-Hardware-Sales-Insights-Dashboard

# What is Purpose?

To unlock the sales insight that are not vision for sales team for decision support and automate them to reduce manual time in data gathering.

# What are stakeholder?

Sales Director

Marketing team

Customer support team

Data and Analystics team

IT

# What is End result?

An automated dashboard providing quick and lastest sales insights in order to support data driven decision making.

# What is the success criteria?

Dashboard(s) uncovering sales order insights with lastest data available.

Sales team able to take better decision and provide 10% cost savings of total spend.

Sales analysts stop data gathering manually in order to save 20% of business time and reinvest it value added activity.
